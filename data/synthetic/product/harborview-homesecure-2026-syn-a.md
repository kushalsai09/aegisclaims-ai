Every provision below is a **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE**. This document is
not copied from, affiliated with, or representative of any real insurer.

## Insured property

The policy applies only to the fictional residence identified on the synthetic
declarations and to detached structures recorded for that location.

## Coverage sections

- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE — Coverage A, Residence:** Direct physical
  damage caused by a listed covered event may be eligible for decision support,
  subject to exclusions, conditions, deductibles, and endorsements.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE — Coverage B, Detached Structures:** The
  fictional limit is 10 percent of the Coverage A limit unless changed by an
  endorsement.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE — Coverage C, Personal Property:** The
  fictional limit is 50 percent of Coverage A; this Phase 2 dataset does not
  make payment or coverage decisions.

## Listed covered events

- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** Wind is a listed event when direct physical
  damage occurs during the policy period.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** Sudden accidental discharge from an
  interior plumbing system is a listed event, but repeated seepage is excluded.

## Exclusions

- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** Wear, deterioration, latent defect, and
  deferred maintenance are excluded causes.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** Flood and surface-water damage are excluded
  unless the synthetic `WATER-SYN-1` endorsement is present.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** Loss occurring outside the effective period
  is not evaluated under this edition.

## Deductibles

- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** The base property deductible is USD 1,500.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** The wind deductible is USD 2,500 when the
  declarations identify the wind-deductible endorsement `WIND-SYN-2`.

## Conditions

  source facts rather than silently select one.

  evidence set is considered complete.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE:** A policy-edition mismatch, material source
  conflict, or explicit mandatory-review indicator requires human review.

## Definitions

- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE — Direct physical damage:** Observable physical
  alteration documented by an authorized synthetic source.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE — Repeated seepage:** Water escaping over 14 or
  more consecutive days.
- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE — Policy period:** The interval beginning on
  the effective date and ending immediately before the expiration date.

## Endorsements

- **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION RULE — WIND-SYN-2:** Replaces the base deductible
  with the USD 2,500 wind deductible for listed wind events.
