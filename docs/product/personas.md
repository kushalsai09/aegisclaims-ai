# Personas

## Claims Adjuster

**Goal:** Understand a claim quickly, find applicable policy evidence, identify gaps, and prepare a defensible next step.

**Needs:** A concise claim overview; provenance-linked facts; side-by-side contradictions; exact policy citations; missing-item checklist; status and limitations; ability to correct AI output.

**Permissions:** Read assigned claims and authorized policy material; start permitted analyses; submit feedback; propose edits; request review. Cannot change system rules, evaluation thresholds, or approve actions reserved for supervisors.

**Failure concerns:** A fluent but unsupported answer, the wrong policy edition, hidden missing documents, lost work, or an opaque escalation.

## Supervisor / Human Reviewer

**Goal:** Resolve escalated cases and approve, reject, or return consequential proposed actions.

**Needs:** The triggering reasons; immutable input snapshot; evidence and citations; rule results; unresolved contradictions; model/prompt/retrieval versions; employee edits; prior review history.

**Permissions:** Everything needed for assigned review queues; edit decision-support artifacts; approve or reject within role; return for more evidence. Cannot suppress audit records or exceed claim/document authorization.

**Failure concerns:** Approval based on stale evidence, unclear changes after escalation, queue duplication, or insufficient separation of duties.

## Compliance / Operations Reviewer

**Goal:** Examine quality, process adherence, recurring failure modes, audit completeness, cost, and operational performance without altering claim outcomes.

**Needs:** Aggregated dashboards, filterable traces, evaluation trends, sampled cases, model/prompt versions, escalation rates, access reports, and exportable audit evidence.

**Permissions:** Read authorized aggregated metrics and sampled records; run approved evaluations; record compliance findings. Claim-level access is separately granted and logged; no default approval authority.

**Failure concerns:** Sensitive-data overexposure, irreproducible outputs, incomplete lineage, misleading aggregate metrics, or silent regressions.

## Accessibility and usability

All personas may use keyboard-only navigation or assistive technology. The primary application targets WCAG 2.2 AA, including visible focus, semantic landmarks, programmatic status announcements, sufficient contrast, non-color-only severity indicators, accessible tables, and review controls that do not rely on drag-and-drop.
